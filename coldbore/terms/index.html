iCloud Backup

iCloud Backup is optional and free. When you turn it on, Coldbore encrypts a copy of your collection on your device and stores it in your own private iCloud storage. It depends on your Apple Account, your available iCloud storage and Apple’s iCloud service, which Apple provides under its own terms.

We do not operate a server, and we cannot see, access or restore your backups for you. Backups run when your device allows, so the latest changes may not be backed up yet. While iCloud Backup is off, new changes are not backed up. If you delete your iCloud backups, or can no longer access your Apple Account, we cannot recover them.

  • The price and billing period are shown before you buy. Any free trial or introductory price, and what you pay after it ends, is also shown before you buy.

  • Your membership renews automatically for the same period unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Your Apple Account is charged for the renewal within the 24 hours before the period ends.
  • You can manage or cancel your membership at any time in your Apple Account settings. Canceling stops the next renewal, and you keep member features until the end of the period you have paid for.

  • If your membership ends, nothing is deleted. Your records stay in Coldbore, and member features lock again until you rejoin.
