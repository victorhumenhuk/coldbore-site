Your records

Coldbore stores your collection records locally. It does not create a Coldbore account or provide a readable online copy of your records.

You can correct a record in the app, prepare an export or local archive, and erase local records from Settings. Erasing local records also removes Coldbore-managed attachments, app-owned archives, temporary export files, Spotlight entries and the attachment key. It cannot remove copies you previously shared or moved elsewhere.

Private Sync and recovery

Private Sync is optional. Once enabled, it automatically uploads encrypted snapshots and encrypted attachment blobs to your private iCloud database. It does not upload readable SwiftData records. Storage uses your iCloud quota. Keep your recovery phrase somewhere you can access without this device.

Keep your recovery phrase safe. Coldbore cannot retrieve a lost recovery phrase. On a new device, enter the phrase, review the encrypted copy and restore it additively. The app does not overwrite existing local records without review.

Membership and purchases

Membership is optional. Your ordinary local records, exports, backup, restore and app lock stay available without it.

Apple handles App Store purchases and cancellation controls. In Coldbore, use Restore App Store purchases to refresh an entitlement for the current App Store account.

What Coldbore does not do

Coldbore is a factual record-keeping app. It does not decide whether an item, storage arrangement, transfer or activity is lawful, safe, insured, properly licensed or compliant. A Collection Pack is a read-only record of information you entered. It is not independent proof, a valuation or an ownership certificate.
